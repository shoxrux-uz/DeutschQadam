Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 16-noyabr

«Musofir» (keyingi o'rinlarda — «ilova») — nemis, rus va ingliz tillarini o'rgatuvchi mobil ilova. Ilova muallifi va ma'lumotlar uchun javobgar shaxs — Shoxrux (jismoniy shaxs, O'zbekiston). Aloqa: musofirapp@gmail.com.

1. Hisobsiz foydalanish

Ilovaning asosiy qismi (darslar, lug'at, testlar, o'yinlar) hisobsiz ishlaydi. Bu holatda o'quv progressingiz (ismingiz, XP, o'rgangan so'zlar, natijalar, sozlamalar) faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi va bizga yuborilmaydi.

2. Google bilan kirganda yig'iladigan ma'lumotlar

Agar o'zingiz «Google bilan kirish»ni tanlasangiz, quyidagilar saqlanadi:

  • Google hisobi ma'lumotlari: Google identifikatori, ismingiz, email manzilingiz, Google profil rasmingiz havolasi;
  • O'quv progressi: XP, daraja, seriya, o'rganilgan so'zlar va bosqichlar (boshqa qurilmada tiklash uchun zaxira);
  • Faollik: ilovaga kirgan kunlaringiz va ilova versiyasi (umumiy statistika uchun);
  • Ijtimoiy funksiyalar: profil nomi va rasmi (siz tanlagan rasm), do'stlik kodi, do'stlaringiz ro'yxati, guruhlar, haftalik XP reytingi, siz yozgan chat xabarlari va siz yuborgan shikoyatlar.

3. Mikrofon va ovoz

Talaffuz mashqida mikrofon faqat siz 🎤 tugmasini bosganingizda yoqiladi. Ovozingiz telefoningizdagi nutqni tanish xizmatiga (odatda Google) matnga aylantirish uchun beriladi. Ilova ovozni yozib olmaydi va saqlamaydi, bizga faqat tanilgan matn keladi va u ham saqlanmaydi.

Insho (Writing) tekshiruvi

Ingliz tili kursida inshoni «Tekshirish» tugmasi bilan yuborsangiz, insho matni va mavzusi baholash uchun Google kompaniyasining Gemini sun'iy intellekt xizmatiga (Google AI Studio API) yuboriladi. Insho matni bizning serverda saqlanmaydi: jadvalda faqat sana, so'zlar soni va olingan ball qayd etiladi. Google'ning bepul tarifida Google bu matnlardan o'z xizmatlarini yaxshilash uchun foydalanishi mumkin (Google shartlariga ko'ra). Inshoga shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni (telefon, manzil va h.k.) yozmang. Natija va insho matni faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi.

«Musofir bilan gaplash» (AI suhbat)

Ingliz tili kursidagi suhbat bo'limida yozgan yoki gapirgan xabaringiz (matn ko'rinishida) va oxirgi xabarlar tarixi javob olish uchun Google Gemini xizmatiga yuboriladi. Suhbat matni bizning serverda saqlanmaydi — faqat kunlik xabarlar soni hisoblanadi; suhbat tarixi telefoningizda turadi. Bepul tarifda Google bu matnlardan o'z xizmatlarini yaxshilash uchun foydalanishi mumkin. Suhbatda shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni yozmang.

4. Bildirishnomalar

Kunlik eslatmalar va do'stlardan xabar bildirishnomalari telefoningizda yaratiladi. Xabar bor-yo'qligini tekshirish uchun ilova serverimizga hisob kalitingiz bilan so'rov yuboradi.

5. Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Serverdagi ma'lumotlar muallifning Google hisobidagi Google Sheets jadvali va Google Apps Script xizmatida (Google serverlarida) saqlanadi. Barcha aloqa shifrlangan (HTTPS) kanal orqali bo'ladi. Har bir hisobga maxfiy kalit beriladi.

6. Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish

  • progressni zaxiralash va boshqa qurilmada tiklash;
  • do'stlar, reyting va chat funksiyalarini ishlatish;
  • ilovadan foydalanish bo'yicha umumiy statistika (necha kishi foydalanmoqda);
  • shikoyatlarni ko'rib chiqish va qoidabuzarlikning oldini olish.

Biz ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz, reklama uchun ishlatmaymiz va uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz (qonun talab qilgan holatlar bundan mustasno). Ilovada reklama va uchinchi tomon analitikasi yo'q.

7. Chat qoidalari

Yozishish faqat bir-birining taklifini qabul qilgan do'stlar va ular tuzgan guruhlar ichida mumkin. Haqoratli so'zlar avtomatik yopiladi. Har qanday foydalanuvchini bloklash va xabar ustidan shikoyat qilish mumkin; shikoyatlar muallif tomonidan ko'rib chiqiladi.

8. Saqlash muddati va o'chirish

Ma'lumotlaringiz hisobingizni o'chirguningizcha saqlanadi. Hisobni o'chirish: ilovada Profil → «Hisobni o'chirish» yoki bu sahifadagi yo'l-yo'riq orqali. O'chirilganda hisob, progress zaxirasi, profil, do'stliklar, guruhlardagi a'zolik, siz yozgan xabarlar va faollik jurnali o'chiriladi. Telefoningizdagi ma'lumot ilovani o'chirganda yoki o'chirish oynasida tanlasangiz o'chadi.

9. Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshdan kichik bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan. Bunday bola ma'lumoti bilib-bilmay saqlanib qolganini bilsak, uni o'chiramiz.

10. O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, shu sahifada yangi sana bilan e'lon qilinadi.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

“Musofir” (the “app”) is a mobile app for learning German, Russian and English in the Uzbek language. The app is developed and operated by Shoxrux, an individual developer in Uzbekistan. Contact: musofirapp@gmail.com.

1. Using the app without an account

Lessons, vocabulary, tests and games work without an account. Your learning progress is then stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Data collected when you sign in with Google

  • Google account data: Google user ID, name, email address, profile picture URL;
  • Learning progress: XP, level, streak, learned words and lessons (backup and restore);
  • Activity: days you used the app and app version (aggregate statistics);
  • Social features: profile name and picture you choose, friend code, friend list, groups, weekly XP leaderboard, chat messages you send and reports you submit.

3. Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap the 🎤 button in pronunciation practice. Audio is passed to your device's speech recognition service (usually Google) to be converted to text. The app does not record or store audio; the recognised text is used only to score your pronunciation and is not stored.

Essay (Writing) feedback

In the English course, when you submit an essay with the "Check" button, the essay text and its topic are sent to Google's Gemini AI service (Google AI Studio API) to be scored. The essay text is not stored on our server; only the date, word count and resulting score are recorded. Under Google's free tier, Google may use this text to improve its services (per Google's terms). Please do not include personal details (phone number, address, etc.) in essays. The result and the essay text are kept only on your phone.

"Talk with Musofir" (AI chat)

In the English course chat, the message you type or speak (as text) and the recent conversation history are sent to Google Gemini to generate a reply. Chat text is not stored on our server — only a daily message count is kept; the conversation history stays on your phone. Under Google's free tier, Google may use this text to improve its services. Please do not share personal details in the chat.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders and friend message notifications are generated on your device. To check for new messages the app sends a request to our server with your account key.

5. Storage and security

Server-side data is stored in Google Sheets and Google Apps Script under the developer's Google account (Google servers). All traffic uses HTTPS. Each account has a private key.

6. How we use data

To back up and restore progress, to provide friends, leaderboard and chat features, to compute aggregate usage statistics, and to review reports and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties except where required by law. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

7. Chat

Messaging is possible only between users who accepted each other's friend request, and in groups created by friends. Offensive words are masked automatically. Users can block others and report messages; reports are reviewed by the developer.

8. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete your account: in the app via Profile → “Delete account”, or as described on the account deletion page. Deletion removes your account, progress backup, profile, friendships, group memberships, messages you sent and activity log.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we hold such data, we will delete it.

10. Changes

Any changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
