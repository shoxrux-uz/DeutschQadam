Musofir hisobini o'chirish

1-usul: ilovaning o'zida (darhol)

  1. Musofir ilovasini oching.
  2. Pastdagi «Profil» bo'limiga o'ting.
  3. «🗑 Hisobni o'chirish» ni bosing va tasdiqlang.

2-usul: ilova o'chirilgan bo'lsa

Google bilan kirgan email manzilingizdan bizga xat yuboring. Mavzu: «Hisobni o'chirish». Hisobingiz 7 kun ichida o'chiriladi va javob xati yuboriladi.

✉️ Xat yozish

Nimalar o'chiriladi

  • Google hisobi bilan bog'langan hisobingiz (ism, email, rasm havolasi);
  • bulutdagi progress zaxirasi;
  • profil, do'stlik kodi, do'stliklar va guruhlardagi a'zolik;
  • siz yozgan chat xabarlari, siz yuborgan shikoyatlar;
  • faollik jurnali.

Hech narsa saqlanib qolmaydi. Telefondagi progress ilovani o'chirganingizda yoki o'chirish oynasida «telefondagini ham tozalash»ni tanlasangiz o'chadi.

Delete your Musofir account

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Musofir app.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom bar).
  3. Tap “🗑 Delete account” and confirm.

Option 2: if you no longer have the app

Email us from the Google account you signed in with, subject “Delete account”. Your account will be deleted within 7 days and you will receive a confirmation.

✉️ Send email

What is deleted

  • your account linked to Google (name, email, picture URL);
  • cloud progress backup;
  • profile, friend code, friendships and group memberships;
  • chat messages you sent and reports you submitted;
  • activity log.

No data is retained. Data stored on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or choose to clear it in the deletion dialog.
